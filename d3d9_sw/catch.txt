$$ Catch only real crashes. Mono raises first-chance access violations as part
$$ of normal null-reference handling, so those break on second chance only.
$$
$$ Commands are inlined rather than kept in a script file: $$>a< inside a quoted
$$ sx command fails to resolve the path and the break then falls through to a
$$ debugger with no input, which quits and takes the process with it.
$$
$$ Every handler is registered for both chances, since the fail-fast codes have
$$ arrived as second chance every time so far.
.echo ===SETUP===
